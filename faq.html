For safety and insurance reasons, no. Even if you’ve been towing big rigs all your life and have the most powerful, coolest truck ever, no.

What time is checkin and checkout?

It depends on the campground, but most don’t allow checking in before 2pm and checkout is usually 11am. I will work with you to establish a convenient checkout time for the camper but I typically need about 1 hour to break down the camper. I ask you please have the camper cleaned out and all personal belongings out of the camper by the contract checkout time.

Do you allow pets?

Yes! I do not allow cats mainly because I have one and I know that cats can be a$$holes. But dogs are awesome and more than welcome as long as you take responsibility for them and any damages they do (chewing, ripping, tearing screens, stains, smells, over cooking bacon).

Check with campgrounds before bringing your dog to make sure you have all documents needed. Pets can cause damage to furniture, flooring, doors, and windows. Only the pet owner knows how their pet will behave unattended so I suggest you minimize the time you leave your pet in the camper while you are not there. Crates are a good way of ensuring that your pet doesn’t get into trouble and can be setup inside the camper.

Any stains, smells, rips, tears, or other damage done by your pet is the renter’s responsibility. I may charge up to a current cleaning fee if there is a difficult-to-clean mess, odors from pets or humans, or glitter left behind in the camper.

What should I do before I check out?

On the last day of your stay please do the following before checkout. Also remember I need time to break down the camper and get it off the site before the campground checkout time. So doing these things before I arrive is key.

  • remove all your personal belongings and double check cabinets and bins to make sure you're not forgetting something
  • remove all food and items from the fridge and pantry (unopened beers or leftover rum is acceptable)
  • please make sure all dishes, cups, pots, silverware that were used are clean, dried, and put away where you found them
  • if you used the grill make sure the surface is cleaned. The Blackstone is best cleaned by heating it up and pouring some water on the surface to push the grease in the tray. Then wipe down with clean paper towel
  • pease sweep the floors to clean up all dirt, sand, leaves, and fur. Vacuum if you brought a fuzzy creature with you. Vacuum can be found under the dinette bench.
  • wipe down the counter tops and surfaces in the bathroom using the all purpose cleaner and/or Lysol wipes
  • make sure the the roof vents are fully closed! If left open and rain enters before pickup you will be charged an extra cleaning fee

Broom, paper towels, general purpose cleaner, and Clorox wipes can be found in the campers for cleaning up. If there are any pet messes PLEASE clean it and let me know as stain and odor remover applied quickly enough can prevent damage and help avoid an additional cleaning fee.

The campers have NEVER been smoked in. If I detect a smoke odor in the camper you will be charged a minimum current cleaning fee. Cushions, mattresses, and other upholstery may also need to be replaced which the renter would also be liable for the cost of replacement.

Do you book the camp site too?
